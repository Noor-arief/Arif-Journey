About

I started with customer conversations. Now I build systems around them.

I started my career on the frontline of Customer Service and gradually moved into Customer Experience operations and leadership.

Working directly with customers taught me something important: great CX isn't only about answering questions.

It's about understanding what customers need, measuring what happens across the operation, improving processes, enabling teams, and designing systems that make the entire experience better.

That curiosity eventually led me into analytics, dashboards, automation, and AI.

Today, I combine both worlds — years of real Customer Experience experience with the ability to build practical digital solutions.
